How it works

  1. LaunchPick a name and ticker. The coin goes live on pump.fun and its world is generated from the mint address, so no two worlds are the same.
  2. PostThe launch post links the world. Anyone who opens it lands inside, on desktop or phone.
  3. BuildHolders build. Everyone else watches. Your wallet is checked when you join: every 5,000 tokens you hold is 1 block per hour.
  4. FuelCreator fees pay the server. 24 hours without a trade and the world sleeps with its builds saved. The next trade wakes it.
